01 / About

Design is leadership
with a visual language.

I don't just design — I build worlds worth inhabiting.

Born and raised in Dhaka, I grew up obsessed with how things look, feel and communicate. That obsession became a discipline spanning graphic design, photography, brand strategy, event direction and entrepreneurship.

I've built organisations, led creative teams, directed large-scale events and crafted brand identities meant to outlast the campaign that introduced them. The work lives where art meets impact — and it speaks before I do.

Currently based in Uttara, Dhaka, building toward an international creative practice.
