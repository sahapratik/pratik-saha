Two worlds.
One eye.

Eighteen frames from the rivers, hill tracts and tea gardens of Bangladesh — slow, wide, weather-driven work where the job is to wait for the light rather than make it. Seventeen from live stages, where the light lasts a fraction of a second and there is no second take.

Every image here ships at full resolution in the viewer. Nothing is cropped to fit a grid — each frame keeps the shape it was taken in.
