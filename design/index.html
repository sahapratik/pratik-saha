01 / The archive

Systems, not one-offs.

Most of this work exists inside a series — a campaign that had to hold across five posts, a brand that had to survive being printed, projected and posted. The individual piece is the visible part; the system underneath it is the job.

The motion work runs on the same logic. Colour, pacing and type are decided once, then applied — which is why a 14-second cut and a 37-second montage still read as coming from the same hand.
